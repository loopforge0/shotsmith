# Static shot

**Status:** verified (one render, seed 7, Kate, fifties diner)
**Effect:** a locked-off camera that never moves, so all the motion is the subject's. For dialogue and performance.
**Why it is here:** Higgsfield's "Static". One of MiniMax's twelve primitives (Static Shot).

## Shot line

> The camera is a static shot, locked off on a tripod for the whole shot: it does not push, pan, tilt or shake, and the framing never changes.
