# Impact shake (effect)

**Status:** testing
**Effect:** a still shot is hit by a huge off-screen impact; the camera shakes strongly and dust falls while the person braces.
**Why it is here:** Shake Strongly is one of MiniMax's twelve primitives; we only had it inside the handheld tracking shot.
