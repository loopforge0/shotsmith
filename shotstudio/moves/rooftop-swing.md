# Rooftop swing (effect)

**Status:** verified (take 2 wording: Mei, rooftop and Lena, neon megacity, seed 7; both turn round properly)
**Why it is here:** A swing from one building to another on a line. A request; the superhero it evokes is not named, in the prompt or the app.

## Take 1 (Lena, neon megacity, seed 7)

The swing worked, but after landing her back became her front instead of her turning round: the prompt never said which way she faced, and its ending asked for her face. Take 2 states it at each stage (back to the lens through the swing and the landing) and makes the turn an action: she stands, steps and pivots through a half turn.
