# Fisheye close-up

**Status:** verified (two renders, judged good by eye)
**Effect:** an ultra-wide fisheye a hand's width from the face, the frame bowed into a barrel, pushing in as the subject leans into the lens.
**Why it is here:** Asked for in the comments. Higgsfield's "Fisheye".

## Take 1 (seed 7, Sofia, rain street)

Named the lens ("ultra-wide fisheye lens", "barrel distortion"). It came back as a plain push-in to a close-up with only slight wide-angle warp, which fits the library's finding that lens names do almost nothing. Take 2 describes what the picture looks like instead.

## Take 2 (seed 11, Lena, neon megacity)

Described the warp instead of naming the lens. Came back as a push-in to an extreme close-up with ordinary wide-angle perspective, no bulge and no curved rim. Neither take shows a strong circular bulge, but both read well by eye as a wide, close push-in with the lens almost on the face, and that was the call. The recipe keeps take 2's wording.
